IDLE credits
============

IDLE was created by Guido van Rossum and has been maintained and
extended by many contributors to Python over the years, among them
the members of the IDLEfork project.  Their work defined the behavior
that this version of IDLE reproduces.

This version of the idlelib package was written for a re-implementation
of Python 3.15 from the published tests and documentation.

Thanks to everyone who reported problems, suggested improvements and
wrote tests -- in São Paulo, Zürich, Kraków, Reykjavík and everywhere
else.
